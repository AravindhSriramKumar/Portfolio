Featured projects

  • EcoVis Framework. A task-aware modality selection is implemented in the framework which chooses the most effective sensing pathway according to utility. This enables more efficient multi-modal inference under edge deployment constraints.
  • Wireless Digital Twins & RTI. A pipeline for spatial understanding that combines structure-from-motion, Gaussian splatting, and SAGD-based segmentation to reconstruct 3D layouts, while estimating material properties through radio tomographic imaging within the ARGOS system.
  • Signal Processing Toolbox. A collection of custom implementations for singular value decomposition, constant false alarm rate filtering and 2D filtering primitives optimized for raw UWB CIR and Wi-Fi CSI processing, designed to support reliable downstream sensing and localization tasks.
  • KLA AI Hackathon, March 2026. Built a high-fidelity image restoration pipeline targeting denoising and super-resolution on the hackathon dataset, with an emphasis on perceptual quality and faithful detail recovery.
